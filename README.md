## 🧠 Overview

**MESHmachine** is a Blender-focused modeling toolkit created for artists who work extensively with detailed hard-surface geometry. Rather than replacing Blender's existing modeling tools, it adds a collection of specialized operations for repairing, reshaping, and refining areas that can become difficult to manage during complex asset development.

The add-on is especially useful when a model contains numerous bevels, chamfers, boolean cuts, and intersecting surfaces. Selected regions can be reconstructed or modified while preserving the surrounding design, allowing artists to make localized changes without repeatedly rebuilding complete sections of an asset.

Its surface-oriented tools provide additional control over how separate forms transition into one another. Operations for fusing surfaces, restoring previous edge treatments, producing controlled fillets, and shaping transitional areas can help create cleaner mechanical forms and more deliberate highlight lines.

Beyond surface reconstruction, **MESHmachine** includes workflows for storing useful mesh states, reusing detailed components, managing custom normals, and handling symmetry-related operations. These capabilities make it a versatile addition to Blender projects involving vehicles, machinery, consumer products, architectural details, sci-fi assets, and precision-oriented 3D design.

## Additional Information for Windows Users

**MESHmachine** is intended for artists who frequently need to revise complicated hard-surface geometry after the initial modeling stage. It provides targeted tools for working with bevels, chamfers, boolean results, transitions, and other areas where standard editing can become time-consuming.

Its reconstruction and surface-transition workflows are useful for mechanical assemblies, vehicles, industrial products, architectural components, and stylized technology assets. Localized operations make it easier to change individual areas while retaining the broader shape of the model.

The Stash and Plug systems also support reusable modeling workflows by preserving useful geometry and providing access to custom surface components that can be inserted into new designs.

**MESHmachine** brings these specialized mesh-editing capabilities together inside Blender, giving hard-surface artists additional control over refinement, detailing, and surface quality.
